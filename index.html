About me

I am a Guest Faculty member at the National Institute of Technology Meghalaya, where I teach C Programming and Software Engineering. I recently completed my Ph.D. at the Centre for Linguistic Science and Technology, IIT Guwahati.

My research sits where vision meets language. For my Ph.D., I built automatic image captioning for Assamese, a low-resource language. This covered dataset creation, transformer models for a morphologically rich language, and data-efficient generation with a pretrained GPT-2. I have also worked on visual question answering and, at Accenture AI Lab, on small vision-language models for chart understanding and numerical reasoning.

I care about AI that works on modest hardware. I design lightweight models and have run a captioning model on a Raspberry Pi 3. My long-term goal is resource-efficient, inclusive multimodal AI for Indian languages.

Ph.D. thesis · IIT Guwahati · 2026

Automatic Image Caption Synthesis for Low-Resource Assamese Language

Two problems hold back image captioning in Assamese: there is very little data, and standard models are not built for a morphologically rich language. My thesis tackles both. It creates and curates several Assamese caption datasets and proposes new transformer architectures. Quantitative and qualitative analysis shows that language-specific design matters for low-resource vision-language generation.

Together, this work establishes the first complete framework for Assamese image captioning.

The thesis in four steps

Train in Assamese, don't translate

Training directly on Assamese captions works better than translating the output of an English captioning model.

J3C5

Build the data

COCO-AC and Flickr30K-AC: large datasets translated from English and manually curated. ChitraLekha10K: a culturally grounded dataset with native-speaker captions.

Design the models

VSSA uses semantic attributes. SPG-TFR adds semantic priors and a spatially encoded transformer. VSB-TFR adds bilinear attention and gives the best results.

J2J1C6

Go pretrained and lightweight

AsCap-GPT2 links visual features to a pretrained Assamese GPT-2 and learns from little data. AC-Lite and VSB-TFR-Lite are built for low-resource devices. AC-Lite runs on a Raspberry Pi 3.
